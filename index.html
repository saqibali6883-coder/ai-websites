Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to set up?

Our activation process is nearly instant. Once payment is confirmed, you will receive your login credentials via email or WhatsApp within 5 to 15 minutes.

Do I need a VPN?

While our service works without a VPN, we recommend one during major sports events to prevent ISP throttling and ensure maximum privacy.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Basic and Gold plans allow one connection at a time. To stream on multiple devices simultaneously, please choose our Platinum Duo plan.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Credit/Debit Cards, PayPal, and Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin/Ethereum) for maximum anonymity.
