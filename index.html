Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our service.

What devices can I use?
We support almost all devices including Firestick, Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony), Android Box, Apple TV, iPhone, Android phones, Mag Boxes, and PCs using VLC or IPTV players.
Do I need a VPN to use LuminaStream?
Our service is VPN-friendly, but not required. We use encrypted streams to ensure your ISP doesn't throttle your connection during major events.
How many channels do I get?
You get access to over 20,000 Live TV channels from around the world, plus a massive VOD library of 60,000+ movies and TV shows, all updated daily.
Can I use one subscription on multiple devices?
Standard subscriptions are for 1 connection at a time. However, you can install it on as many devices as you like, just don't stream simultaneously. Multi-room plans are available upon request.
