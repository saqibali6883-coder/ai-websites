Which devices are supported?

Our service works on all major devices including Amazon Firestick, Android Boxes, Smart TVs (Samsung/LG), Apple TV, iOS, Android Smartphones, and PC/Laptops.

How long does activation take?

Typically, your account is activated within 15-30 minutes of purchase. You will receive an email and WhatsApp message with your login credentials and setup instructions.

Do I need a VPN?

While our service has built-in anti-block technology, using a VPN can provide an extra layer of privacy. Our service is fully compatible with all major VPN providers.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

The Basic and Standard plans allow 1 active connection. The Annual Elite plan supports up to 2 concurrent connections at once.
