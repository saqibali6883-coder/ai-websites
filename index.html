Frequently Asked Questions

What devices are supported?
We support all devices including Amazon Firestick, Android TV Boxes, Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony), MAG boxes, Smartphones (iOS/Android), and PC/Laptop.
What internet speed do I need?
For HD streaming, we recommend at least 15 Mbps. For 4K Ultra HD streaming, a stable connection of 25 Mbps or higher is ideal.
Can I use the service on multiple devices?
Our standard plans allow for one connection at a time. If you wish to watch on multiple devices simultaneously, please contact us for multi-room pricing.
How long does activation take?
Once payment is confirmed, activation typically takes between 15 to 30 minutes. You will receive login instructions via email or WhatsApp.
